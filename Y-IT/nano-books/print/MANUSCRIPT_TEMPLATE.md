## WRITING STYLE GUIDE

### Tone

**DO:**
- ✅ Be satirical but empathetic ("beautiful, stubborn fool")
- ✅ Use dark humor (corporate dystopia meets comedy)
- ✅ Back every claim with data
- ✅ Offer actionable alternatives (not just negativity)
- ✅ Use specific examples (not vague generalizations)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Mock the victims (only mock the gurus)
- ❌ Use motivational fluff
- ❌ Offer false hope
- ❌ Be cruel (be honest, not mean)
- ❌ Use jargon without explanation

### Voice Examples

**Good:**
"You're not stupid. You're optimistic. The difference is, stupid people don't read books analyzing their business ideas before starting. You're here. That's smart. Now let's use that intelligence to avoid a $4,832 mistake."

**Bad:**
"Only an idiot would try dropshipping in 2024."

**Good:**
"The guru showed you his Shopify dashboard with $47,382 in sales. He didn't show you the $51,204 in costs, the 80-hour work weeks, or the divorce papers."

**Bad:**
"Gurus are all scammers and you're dumb for listening."

### Statistical Citation

**Always provide sources:**
- "According to [Source], [X]% of [business model] businesses fail within [timeframe]."
- "Data from [Study] shows average first-year losses of $[amount]."
- "Industry analysis by [Company/Researcher] found [specific stat]."

**If source is anecdotal:**
- "Based on interviews with [X] former [business owners]..."
- "Case study analysis of [X] businesses revealed..."

### Formatting

**Use:**
- Short paragraphs (3-5 sentences max)
- Bullet points for lists
- Bold for emphasis (sparingly)
- Italics for guru quotes (satirical contrast)
- Em dashes for emphasis — like this
- Subheadings every 200-300 words

**Avoid:**
- Walls of text
- Excessive exclamation points!!!
- ALL CAPS (except for emphasis in tables/charts)
- Overly complex sentences
